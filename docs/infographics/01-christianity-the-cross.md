## Purpose (one sentence)

Show, at a glance, Christianity's central and most audacious move: taking a Roman
instrument of public execution and turning it into the sign of hope.

## The idea to communicate

From the site's own prose: *"The central symbol is an instrument of torture…
Christianity's boldest move is to take the worst thing the empire could do to a
body and make it the sign of hope."* The graphic must carry a **turn** — a
before/after, an inversion. Not "here is a cross" but "here is a death-machine
that became a promise."

Three plain facts a reader should leave with:
1. The cross was a **Roman method of execution** (crucifixion), not originally a
   religious emblem.
2. Christianity **reversed its meaning** — from shame/death to hope/resurrection.
3. It is now worn, raised over altars, and marked on the body — the tradition's
   single most recognisable sign.

## The photo (the "window")

- **Subject:** one real cross as a *material object* — a weathered wooden or
  stone cross, a crucifix worn at the throat, a cross silhouetted against a
  window. Prefer texture and age over a shiny new object. Human presence is
  welcome but not required (a hand holding a small cross reads beautifully).
- **Placement:** upper two-thirds, full-bleed to the left and top edges, so the
  title block can sit in a darkened lower third.
- **Treatment:** duotone toward Christianity — shadows to `bg #0c0a09`,
  highlights to amber `#f59e0b` / `#fbc86b`. Film grain ~4%. The cross should
  look **lit by a single warm ember**, emerging from near-black.
- Sourcing: see the "Christianity · The Cross" lens set in
  [`photo-picker.html`](photo-picker.html).

│  │ EXECUTION│  →  │  HOPE    │ │     left chip muted, right chip amber
│  └─────────┘      └─────────┘ │
│                               │

The load-bearing device is the **EXECUTION → HOPE turn**: the left chip is set
in `muted` on `surface` (ash, dead); the arrow and right chip glow `accent`. The
whole graphic is that one arrow.

## Type

- Kicker: `SYMBOL · CHRISTIANITY` — sans, `#f59e0b`, uppercase, `+0.22em`.
- Title: `The Cross` — serif 500, `#e7e5e4`, tracking `−0.02em`.
- Chips: sans 500, 22px. Left `EXECUTION` in `#a8a29e`; right `HOPE` in `#f59e0b`.
- Body caption: serif 400, `#e7e5e4`, one or two sentences max.

## Iconography

The **cross** glyph as an 8% amber watermark bleeding off the top-right corner —
subtle, behind the photo's darkest region. Do not add a second symbol.

## Piece-specific don'ts

- Don't make it gory or literal about crucifixion — the power is in the *reversal*,
  restraint carries it.
- Don't turn the arrow into a "conversion funnel" or marketing arrow — it's a
  semantic turn, keep it quiet (a thin line + arrowhead in accent).

## Accessibility & alt text

Ensure the title and chips sit on a region darkened to ≥4.5:1. Ship this alt text:

> Alt: "A weathered cross lit by a single warm light against near-black. A
> diagram beside it reads 'Execution' pointing with an arrow to 'Hope',
> summarising how Christianity turned a Roman instrument of death into its sign
> of hope."
