# Infographic 05 — Word & Pattern

## Purpose (one sentence)

Show Islam's refusal of figurative religious images, and how that same creative
force poured instead into two channels: **the word** (calligraphy) and **the
pattern** (geometry).

## The idea to communicate

From the prose: *"much of the tradition turned away from figurative religious art
and poured that same creative force into two channels instead: the word and the
pattern… where other traditions paint faces, Islam builds geometry."*

A reader should leave with:
1. A **refusal** at the root — wariness that images slide into idolatry.
2. That refusal **redirected**, not suppressed, into two art forms.
3. **Word** = Arabic calligraphy of the Qur'an raised into architecture.
4. **Pattern** = endless interlacing geometry — "a unity with no centre and no
   edge because it is everywhere at once."

## The photo (the "window")

- **Subject (aniconic):** real **Arabic calligraphy** carved/tiled on
  architecture, *or* a real **geometric tile pattern** (zellij, muqarnas, a
  star-lattice screen). This is one of the few pieces where the photo and the
  data device are the same visual language.
- **Placement:** the photo can be a strong full-bleed field (top or full-left),
  with the two-channel structure laid over/beside it.
- **Treatment:** emerald duotone; because the source is already
  pattern/architecture, keep detail crisp — don't blur the geometry. Grain ~3%.
- Sourcing: "Islam · Word & Pattern" lens set in
  [`photo-picker.html`](photo-picker.html).

## Composition — the fork

```
┌──────────────────────────────┐
│        T H E   W I N D O W    │  ← calligraphy or tile photo, full-bleed top
│ ───────────────────────────  │
│  SENSORY WORLD · ISLAM        │  ← kicker emerald
│  Word & Pattern               │  ← serif title
│                               │
│      ┌── a refusal ──┐        │  ← single origin node: "no figurative image"
│      │  no figures   │        │
│      └───────┬───────┘        │
│         ┌────┴────┐           │  ← forks into two channels
│    ┌────▼───┐  ┌──▼─────┐     │
│    │  WORD  │  │ PATTERN│     │
│    │calligra│  │geometry│     │
│    │ -phy   │  │endless │     │
│    └────────┘  └────────┘     │
│  Source · review status       │
└──────────────────────────────┘
```

The device is a **fork**: one refusal at the top splitting into two channels.
This makes the point that the art is *redirection*, not absence. Render the fork
lines in the emerald tonal ramp; the two channel panels use a fine geometric
fill (word panel = a calligraphic flourish motif; pattern panel = a star lattice).

## Type

- Kicker: `SENSORY WORLD · ISLAM`, sans emerald, `+0.22em`.
- Title: `Word & Pattern`, serif 500, `#ecfdf5`.
- Origin node: `A REFUSAL`, sans 500, 13px, `#34d399`, UPPERCASE; sub-line serif
  `no image of God or prophet` in `#89b3a3`.
- Channel headers: `WORD` / `PATTERN`, sans 500, 20px, `#ecfdf5`.
- Channel bodies: serif 400, 15px, `#89b3a3`.

## Iconography

Geometry *is* the iconography here. Use an authentic 8- or 16-point Islamic star
construction as the lattice. Optional small star-and-crescent by the kicker. No
figurative glyphs.

## Piece-specific don'ts

- **Don't** use any human/animal figure, even stylised.
- Don't present the refusal as a prohibition sign / "no-entry" icon — it's a
  turning-toward, not just a banning. Keep it dignified.
- Don't use fake or decorative "Arabic-looking" squiggles — if calligraphy is
  drawn (not photographed), it must be real, correctly-formed Arabic script,
  reviewed by someone literate in it.

## Accessibility & alt text

Keep the lattice/flourish fills low-contrast enough that overlaid text stays
