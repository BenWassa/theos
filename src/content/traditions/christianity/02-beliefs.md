Strip Christianity to its load-bearing beams and a few ideas hold up the rest.

**One God in three persons.** Father, Son, and Spirit, not three gods, not three masks, but one being in an eternal relationship of love. The doctrine is deliberately difficult; it insists that God is, in the deepest sense, *relational* rather than solitary.

**God became human.** In Jesus of Nazareth the tradition claims that God took on a full human life without ceasing to be God. This is the hinge. It means the divine is not repelled by the body, by suffering, or by death.

**Death is not the end of the story.** The resurrection of Jesus is not offered as a metaphor but as a reversal, the claim that love has proven stronger than the grave, and that the same is promised to those who follow.

**Grace, not merit.** Rescue is received, not earned. A person is made right with God as a gift, and the moral life is the grateful response to that gift rather than the price of it.

Everything else, the sacraments, the ethics, the hope of a world made new, hangs from these.
